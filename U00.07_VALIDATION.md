## Fifteen focused control groups

1. Default/missing controls and environment-only live attempts fail closed;
   direct OpenAI factory invocation remains blocked.
2. Valid explicit policy and rejection of missing/negative/unbounded budget/rate/
   proof/price/model/lease/identity settings; no positive spending defaults.
3. Two competing requests with one paid reservation's budget produce at most
   one paid authorization/dispatch.
4. Request replay and repeated settlement cannot redispatch or double-refund.
5. Insufficient paid capacity and deliberately disabled paid path use only the
   eligible fallback with truthful provider/model/mode/reason.
6. Fallback independent day/month request, money and concurrency limits, including
   daily rollover distinct from monthly exhaustion; no retry to paid.
7. Independent visitor/global windows and shared cross-mode anonymous limits.
8. Global/client/path concurrency; expired leases free slots but retain exposure.
9. UTC month rollover attributes outstanding settlement to the original month.
10. Missing usage and late/expired receipts never refund; proven excess debits
    once and blocks; duplicate overrun receipts do not charge twice.
11. Provider exception and ambiguous timeout keep full exposure; late completion
    cannot settle/refund again; no provider-error fallback.
12. Durable-port failure, timeout, malformed reply and possibly committed timeout
    deny dispatch with no memory bypass/new-ID authorization retry.
13. Invalid input/oversize and invalid AI shape/extra fields/unknown or duplicate
    citations remain rejected through the router.
14. Fixed transport identity, known usage settlement, strict durable protocol and
    safe counter records; no credential/source/raw-IP in records/status/errors.
15. Source/import-graph test rejects public low-level bypasses, APIs/actions and
    production imports of the fake store.

The suites use mocked transports and a fake atomic store only. Test approval/
open-weight evidence URLs/models/token rates are invented fixtures, not real
provider/model/pricing approval. A global rejecting-fetch guard recorded **zero

Sensitive sentinel strings were checked against captured logs and safe failures;

## Remaining live requirements and limitations

The Redis wrapper is a typed atomic-operation **contract**, not an implemented/
connected Redis Lua executor. Live use requires a real approved durable service,
multi-instance atomicity/durability/failover/recovery tests and safe initialization.
The fake's process-local atomicity is not production-safe; no real store is silently
replaced by it. Cost reservations rely on approved rates and conservative token/
wire-overhead assumptions, not an exact invoice guarantee. Unknown/expired spend
is retained; overshoot blocks the affected path, but provider billing backstops
and reconciliation are still required. Lease expiry does not prove provider work
actually stopped. Retention is bounded; old absent records are not proof of zero cost.

Live activation still requires all gates in the
[authoritative control contract](docs/contracts/U00.07_AI_Budget_Routing_Contract.md):
ADR-012 exact paid/open-weight models and rates/terms, independent credentials,
approved budgets/quotas/concurrency, real shared atomic store, trusted anonymous
edge/HMAC secret, protected entrypoint, provider spend backstops, monitoring,
bounded live test budget, end-to-end acceptance and explicit G5 deployment approval.
Neither existing live guard was removed. These are pending activation decisions,
not blockers to this offline PR. No ADR was marked approved.

under `lib/server/ai/`; minimal existing adapter/contracts/SDK transport changes;
