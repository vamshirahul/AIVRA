## Decisions, limitations and rollback

The configured OpenAI live path has two hard stops: pre-dispatch rejection and an
unconditional blocking provider fetch. U00.07 must deliberately replace both
after enforceable budget/rate/concurrency controls and ADR-012 selection.
The generic internal transport seam is trusted server code for tests/provider
composition, not authorization or a client-callable function.

Schema/source references do not prove narrative accuracy or SME approval.
Detailed business schemas/prompts, fallback/routing/budget/cost behavior and
result/session persistence are deferred. No live compatibility/model-quality
test occurred; no provider credential was required for tests or build.
