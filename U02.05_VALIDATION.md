## Contract and validation choices

Canonical `ala/review-export/v1` is independent of frozen `ala/v1`. Artifact is
ala_review_export / exported_unapproved / ala. Full original projection remains in
analysis, separate from validated visitor overlay and ordered effective actions.
Counts distinguish sources/findings/original actions/overrides/missing/not-evaluated.
Provenance and source revision are copied exactly; no invented session ID, app digest,
reviewer/approval/export timestamp. Consumer exposes effective values with original
actions/evidence/rationale/provenance and incomplete status retained. Parsing is only
structural validation, never provider execution proof or import-to-trusted-AI authority.

Strict builder/reader recheck review identity, membership/side/destination, duplicates,
same-subject conflicts, evidence pointers/types/captured-value consistency, source
accounting, provenance shape, counts and derived actions. Malformed/unknown/version/
stale/absent/invalid/outdated data fails without partial output; no repair or inference.
Unmodified AI actions are not human approvals; incomplete remains incomplete even
when all records are structurally accounted. Rationales and strings are never truncated.

File cap **1,000,000 UTF-8 bytes**, separate from original generated-data <=64,000
bytes and unchanged 128,000-input/2,048-token AI limits. CSV may be larger through
repeated evidence: a valid JSON/safe oversized CSV case confirms explicit failure,
not truncation. CSV contains an analysis metadata row even with zero action rows;
quoted RFC4180 cells, doubled quotes/CRLF, formula-like text prefixed with apostrophe.
That safety representation can alter dangerous CSV text; JSON retains exact values.
Unsupported raw C0 controls fail CSV safely. No CSV import/consumer parser is added.
