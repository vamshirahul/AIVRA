transport does not enforce real model token generation: these measurements **do
not establish a complete 50+50 application response under 2,048 tokens**. No budget,
token bound, model selection or silent chunking changed. A future capacity/chunking
design needs scoped review if provider-quality work shows it necessary.

accounting, not recommendation truth/approval. Evidence pointers include nullable
attributes and do not prove a claim. U02.02 must validate against captured source,
